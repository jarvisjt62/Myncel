## Segment 2: Hospitals & Healthcare Facilities

### US Hospital Segments & Target Facilities

**Community Hospitals (Primary Target - Quick Decisions)**
- 150-400 bed facilities
- Independent or small system affiliation
- Facilities director typically makes software decisions
- Target: 500+ facilities across US

**Critical Access Hospitals (< 25 beds)**
- Rural facilities
- Generator reliability critical
- Often lack dedicated maintenance software
- Target: 1,300+ facilities nationally

**Ambulatory Surgery Centers**
- 5,500+ facilities nationally
- HVAC and sterilization equipment focus
- Compliance documentation needs
- Quick decision cycles
