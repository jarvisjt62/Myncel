# Apollo.io: How to Find Your Uploaded CSV

## The Problem
After uploading a CSV, it might not show up where you expect. Here's exactly where to find it.

---

## Method 1: Check Under "Lists" (Most Common)

1. Click **"Lists"** in the left sidebar menu
2. Look for a tab called **"Accounts"** (NOT "Contacts")
3. Your companies should be there under the list name you gave during import
4. If you don't see it, click the dropdown at top to change from "People" to "Accounts"

> ⚠️ **Important:** Apollo has TWO list types - "People" and "Accounts"
> - **Accounts** = Companies (this is where your CSV went)
> - **People** = Individual contacts
> Your CSV has companies, so it's under **Accounts**

---

## Method 2: Easier Approach - Skip the CSV Upload Entirely!

The CSV upload imports companies, but what you really want is **people's emails**. Here's a faster way:

### Step-by-Step (No CSV needed):

1. Click **"Search"** → **"People"** in the left menu
2. In the filters panel on the left, add these filters:

**For Metal Fabrication contacts:**
- Job Title: `Operations Manager` OR `Plant Manager` OR `Maintenance Manager`
- Company Name: type `Protolabs` (or any company from your list)
- Location: `United States`
- Click **"Search"**

3. You'll see actual people with their job titles
4. Click the person → Click **"Save"** → Add to a new list called "Metal Fab Prospects"
5. Click **"Reveal Email"** to get their verified email

### Repeat for each company:
Just change the Company Name filter and search again. Add each person to your list.

---

## Method 3: Search by Industry (Bulk Approach)

This is the FASTEST way to get 50 contacts:

1. Click **"Search"** → **"People"**
2. Add filters:

**For Metal Fabrication:**
- Job Title: `Operations Manager` OR `Plant Manager`
- Industry: `Machinery` OR `Metal Fabrication` OR `Manufacturing`
- Company Size: `51-200` OR `201-500`
- Location: `United States`

**For Healthcare:**
- Job Title: `Facilities Manager` OR `Biomedical Engineering`
- Industry: `Hospital & Health Care`
- Company Size: `1001-5000` OR `5001-10000`
- Location: `United States`

**For Warehouses:**
- Job Title: `Operations Manager` OR `Facilities Manager`
- Industry: `Logistics & Supply Chain` OR `Warehousing`
- Company Size: `201-500` OR `501-1000`
- Location: `United States`

3. Click **"Search"** - you'll get dozens of results
4. Select all (checkbox at top)
5. Click **"Save"** → Add to list
6. Click **"Save & Email"** if you want to email directly from Apollo

## Method 4: Using the CSV (If You Want to Try Again)

1. Go to **"Lists"** → Click **"+ Create List"**
2. Name it "Myncel Prospects"
3. Click **"Import"** → **"Import Accounts"** (not contacts!)
4. Upload MASTER_PROSPECT_LIST_APOLLO.csv
5. Map the columns:
   - "Company Name" → Account Name
   - "Industry Segment" → Industry
   - "Location" → Location
   - "Website" → Website
6. Click **"Import"**
7. Now go back to Lists → Click your new list
8. Click the **"Accounts"** tab (not "People")
9. You should see your companies!
10. Click a company → Click **"People"** tab → Find the decision maker → **"Reveal Email"**

---

## Quick Cheat Sheet

| What You Want | Where to Go |
|---------------|-------------|
| Find uploaded CSV | Lists → Your List Name → **Accounts** tab |
| Find people at a company | Search → People → Filter by Company Name |
| Find people by industry | Search → People → Filter by Industry |
| Get someone's email | Click their profile → "Reveal Email" |
| Save people to a list | Click "Save" on their profile |
| Email directly from Apollo | Lists → Select people → "Save & Email" |
