# Apollo.io Setup Guide - Get Verified Emails in 5 Minutes

## Step 1: Create Free Account

1. Go to **https://www.apollo.io**
2. Click "Sign Up Free"
3. Enter your email and create password
4. Verify your email

**Free Tier Includes:**
- 50 email credits/month
- 50 phone credits/month
- Unlimited searches

---

## Step 2: Upload Your Prospect List

1. Download **MASTER_PROSPECT_LIST_APOLLO.csv** from this folder
2. In Apollo, go to **Import → CSV Import**
3. Upload the CSV file
4. Map columns (Apollo will auto-detect)
5. Click "Import"

Your 50+ companies are now in Apollo!

---

## Step 3: Find Decision-Maker Emails

### Method A: Search by Company
1. Go to **Search → Companies**
2. Type company name (e.g., "Protolabs")
3. Click on the company
4. Go to **People** tab
5. Filter by Job Title: "Operations Manager" or "Plant Manager"
6. Click the person's name
7. Click **Reveal Email** (uses 1 credit)

### Method B: Bulk Enrich (Recommended)
1. Go to your imported list (Lists → select your list)
2. Click **Enrich**
3. Select "Add new contacts"
4. Set filters:
   - Job Title contains: "Operations Manager" OR "Plant Manager" OR "Facilities Manager"
   - Location: United States
5. Click "Enrich"
6. Apollo will find matching contacts and add them to your list

---

## Step 4: Export Emails

1. Go to your enriched list
2. Click **Export**
3. Select fields to include:
   - First Name
   - Last Name
   - Email
   - Company
   - Job Title
   - LinkedIn URL
4. Export as CSV
5. You now have verified emails ready to use!

---

## Step 5: Send Emails

You can now:
- **Use Apollo's email sender** (built-in, sequences available)
- **Export to your email tool** (Gmail, Outlook, Mailchimp, etc.)
- **Copy/paste individual emails** for personal outreach
