## How to Handle Replies

### If They Say "Yes, Tell Me More":
> "Great! I'd love to show you how Myncel works. Do you have 15 minutes this [Tuesday/Wednesday]? Here's my calendar link: [your link]"

### If They Say "Not Right Now":
> "No problem at all, [First Name]. I'll check back in a few months. Feel free to try Myncel free anytime at myncel.com/signup. Best of luck with your operations!"

### If They Ask "How Much Does It Cost?":
> "Great question! We offer flexible pricing based on your team size and number of assets. Most shops our size start around $[price]/month. Would you like me to put together a quote specific to your setup?"

### If They Say "We Already Use [Competitor]":
> "That makes sense! Many of our customers switched from [competitor/system] because they needed [specific advantage - simpler setup, better mobile, industry-specific features]. Would you be open to a quick comparison?"
