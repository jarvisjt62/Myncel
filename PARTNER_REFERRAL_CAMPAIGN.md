# Partner & Referral Outreach Campaign

## Executive Summary

This document outlines a comprehensive partner and referral acquisition strategy to expand Myncel's market reach across manufacturing, healthcare, and warehouse/logistics sectors through strategic partnerships and customer referrals.

---

## 1. PARTNER PROGRAM STRUCTURE

### 1.1 Partnership Tiers

#### Tier 1: Referral Partners
- **Commission**: 15% of first-year revenue from referred clients
- **Requirements**: Signed partner agreement, basic product training
- **Support**: Co-branded materials, partner portal access
- **Ideal For**: Consultants, trade associations, industry influencers

#### Tier 2: Certified Partners
- **Commission**: 20% of first-year revenue + 5% renewal
- **Requirements**: Product certification, minimum 2 referrals/year
- **Support**: Dedicated partner manager, priority support, co-marketing budget
- **Ideal For**: Service companies, equipment distributors, implementation firms

#### Tier 3: Strategic Partners
- **Revenue Share**: Negotiated based on integration depth
- **Requirements**: Technical integration, joint go-to-market plan
- **Support**: Executive sponsor, product roadmap input, joint sales training
- **Ideal For**: ERP providers, IoT sensor companies, major distributors

## 3. OUTREACH STRATEGY

### 3.1 Phase 1: Partner Identification & Qualification (Weeks 1-4)

#### Week 1-2: Research & List Building
- Research top 50 partners per segment using LinkedIn, industry directories
- Identify key contacts (Business Development, Partnerships, Sales Directors)
- Qualify partners based on:
  - Customer base overlap
  - Geographic alignment
  - Strategic fit
  - Partnership history with similar companies

#### Week 3-4: Initial Outreach
- Send personalized partnership inquiry emails
- Connect on LinkedIn with partnership message
- Follow up via phone for high-priority partners

---

### 3.2 Phase 2: Partnership Development (Weeks 5-12)

#### Partner Onboarding Process
1. **Initial Meeting** (30 min)
   - Understand partner's business model
   - Identify mutual customer opportunities
   - Discuss partnership structure

2. **Product Demo** (45 min)
   - Full platform walkthrough
   - Segment-specific capabilities
   - Q&A with partner team

3. **Agreement & Setup** (1-2 weeks)
   - Partner agreement execution
   - Portal access and training
   - Co-branded materials creation

4. **Joint Planning** (30 min)
   - Target customer list development
   - Outreach strategy alignment
   - Success metrics agreement

---

### 3.3 Phase 3: Active Partner Management (Ongoing)

#### Monthly Partner Activities
- Partner newsletter with product updates, success stories
- Co-marketing opportunity identification
- Referral pipeline review
- Feedback collection and product input

#### Quarterly Partner Reviews
- Performance against targets
- Commission payments and reporting
- Strategic planning for next quarter
- Co-marketing campaign development

## 10. APPENDIX: PARTNER AGREEMENT SUMMARY

### Key Terms
- **Agreement Duration**: 12 months, auto-renewing
- **Exclusivity**: Non-exclusive
- **Territory**: As agreed (typically national or regional)
- **Commission Payment**: Within 30 days of referred customer payment
- **Intellectual Property**: Each party retains own IP; co-branded materials jointly owned
- **Termination**: 30-day notice; commissions continue for existing referrals

### Partner Obligations
- Maintain product knowledge through certification
- Represent Myncel accurately and professionally
- Refer customers only in agreed territories
- Report competitive intelligence and market feedback

### Myncel Obligations
- Provide training and certification resources
- Deliver partner support within agreed SLAs
- Process commissions accurately and timely
- Provide co-marketing support as agreed
