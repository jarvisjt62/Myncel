## 5. EMAIL BEST PRACTICES

### Timing Guidelines
- Send Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10 AM local time
- Avoid Monday mornings and Friday afternoons
- For manufacturing, avoid end-of-month (closing pressure)
- For healthcare, avoid Monday (post-weekend catch-up)

### Personalization Checklist
- [ ] Company name in subject line
- [ ] Specific reference to their operation/capabilities
- [ ] Relevant case study or example
- [ ] Correct title and department for contact

### Follow-Up Rules
- Never send more than 7 emails in a sequence
- Always provide value in each touch
- Give clear "out" options (e.g., "If timing isn't right...")
- Track opens and clicks to adjust timing

### Subject Line Testing
- Test 2-3 variants for each segment
- Keep under 50 characters when possible
- Avoid ALL CAPS and excessive punctuation
- Questions often outperform statements

### Compliance Notes
- Include unsubscribe option in all emails
- Honor opt-outs within 10 business days
- Include physical business address
- CAN-SPAM and GDPR compliance required
