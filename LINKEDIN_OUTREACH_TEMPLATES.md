## BEST PRACTICES

### Connection Request Tips:
- Keep under 300 characters
- Personalize with company name and their role
- Ask a question when possible (increases acceptance rate)
- Mention a relevant benefit or statistic

### Follow-Up Timing:
- Send first follow-up 3-5 days after connection
- Second follow-up after 7-10 days
- Third (final) follow-up after 14 days

### Response Rate Optimization:
- Best days: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
- Best times: 8-10 AM and 4-6 PM (recipient's timezone)
- Avoid Monday mornings and Friday afternoons

### Profile Optimization:
Before sending connection requests, ensure your profile shows:
- Clear headline mentioning what you do
- Recent activity/posts about your industry
- Professional photo
- Summary that establishes credibility
